# Welcome to the Blog

Welcome. I am Arshad Ali, and this blog is where I share practical insights from building
and evolving data and analytics platforms, with a strong focus on Microsoft Fabric and
Spark Runtime.

Alongside these engineering topics, I also write about product management: framing customer
problems, evaluating trade-offs, and connecting technical decisions to product outcomes.

If you are a data engineer, analytics architect, product manager, or technical leader,
this space is designed to help you make better platform and product decisions with confidence.

Here is what you can expect from upcoming posts:

- Microsoft Fabric Spark Runtime updates explained in plain technical terms
- Performance and reliability guidance for real-world Spark workloads
- Delta Lake, SQL, Python, and streaming improvements that matter in production
- Data platform modernization patterns across Fabric and Azure Synapse
- Practical perspective on AI-ready data foundations and agentic analytics scenarios
- Product management frameworks for prioritization, product strategy, and technical decision-making
- System design for product managers, including interview preparation and practical examples

For a starting point on product management, explore the
[system design framework for product managers]({% post_url 2026-11-14-system-design-framework-product-managers %}),
which walks through the design process using Google Calendar and a WhatsApp-style messaging app.

My goal is to publish content that is both strategic and hands-on, so you can use it to
guide architecture, improve operations, shape product decisions, and accelerate delivery.

Thank you for reading, and I hope these posts become a useful part of your engineering
and product management playbook.
